Common Questions

Before you book

Do I need a referral to book?

No — you can book directly for any primary care visit. Referrals are only needed for certain specialists.

Can I be seen the same day?

We hold same-day slots each morning for urgent, non-emergency issues. Call ahead to check availability.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of current medications, if you're not transferring records to us.

Is telehealth available?

Yes, for follow-ups and certain consults. Your provider will let you know if a visit qualifies.

Most weeks have room this week.

Book online in under two minutes, or call the front desk if you'd rather talk it through.
